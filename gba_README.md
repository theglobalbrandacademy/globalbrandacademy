## Our approach

GBA follows four stages, from insight to organisation-wide impact. Read more on the [our approach](https://theglobalbrandacademy.com/our-approach) page.

1. **Inspire:** spark new thinking and align people around a future-ready direction.
2. **Build:** develop practical capability and turn insight into action.
3. **Embed:** bring learning into daily behaviour and execution.
4. **Scale:** expand capability for sustained, organisation-wide impact.
